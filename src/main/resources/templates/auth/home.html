What this milestone provides

Authentication, session management, and role-based authorization. The pages that use the STUDENT, REVIEWER, and ADMIN roles arrive with the submission and review milestones; the route rules that will guard them are already in force and are covered by the integration tests.

Your role is read from your account on every request. Disabling the account takes effect on your next request, because the session holds server-side state rather than a self-contained token.
